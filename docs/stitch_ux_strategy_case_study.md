# Iqra (إقرا) - UX Strategy & Case Study

## Project Overview
Iqra is a premium AI Reading Assistant designed for users with reading difficulties, including illiterate individuals and the elderly. The goal is to provide independence in navigating the digital world by making any on-screen text understandable through voice, simple explanations, and translation.

## User Personas
### 1. Amma Fatima (The Elderly User)
*   **Age:** 72
*   **Literacy:** Limited
*   **Tech Literacy:** Low (uses WhatsApp for voice notes only)
*   **Pain Point:** Struggles to read government SMS alerts or medicine instructions on her phone.
*   **Goal:** To understand messages without asking her grandchildren for help.

### 2. Ahmed (The Low-Literacy Worker)
*   **Age:** 34
*   **Literacy:** Basic reading skills, struggles with complex terminology.
*   **Tech Literacy:** Moderate.
*   **Pain Point:** Gets anxious when faced with long terms and conditions or banking apps.
*   **Goal:** Quick, simple explanations of text to perform daily tasks confidently.

## Information Architecture
1. **Onboarding:** Voice-guided walkthrough, permission granting (Screen Overlay), and gesture training.
2. **The Heart (Floating Button):** Persistent, draggable AI companion.
3. **Reading Mode:** Active state when the user selects text or a region of the screen.
4. **Result Dashboard:** 
    *   **Listen:** High-quality text-to-speech.
    *   **Explain:** AI-driven simplification of complex text.
    *   **Translate:** Simple translation into preferred dialect/language.
5. **History & Favorites:** Saved insights for recurring needs (e.g., bills).
6. **Settings:** Voice speed, contrast, and font size controls.

## Design Principles
*   **Zero Anxiety:** No hidden menus or complex gestures.
*   **Voice-First:** Every action is confirmed or guided by a warm, human voice.
*   **High Contrast & Scale:** AAA accessibility standards.
*   **Subtle Motion:** Animations guide the eye to the next action without being distracting.

## Navigation Map
*   **Primary:** Floating Button (Overlay).
*   **Secondary:** Bottom Sheet for results.
*   **Tertiary:** Full-screen App for History, Settings, and Help.

## Accessibility Guidelines
*   Minimum touch target: 56x56dp.
*   Color Contrast: 7:1 for body text.
*   Font: Be Vietnam Pro / Noto Sans Arabic (Large scale).
*   Tactile Feedback: Distinct haptics for success, error, and active reading.
